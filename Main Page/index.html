About Us
I’m a third-year B.Tech Computer Science student at IPU, currently exploring Data Structures and Algorithms, problem-solving, and software development. As I continue learning, I wanted to build something that makes these concepts easier to understand, not just for myself, but for other students as well. Algorithms can sometimes feel confusing when explained only through theory or code. Understanding how they work internally is just as important as knowing how to implement them. That’s where this platform comes in. The goal is simple: break down complex algorithms into simple explanations and interactive visualizations. From sorting and searching to graph traversal and pathfinding, this platform aims to help learners understand each step of an algorithm and see how different approaches solve a problem. You can explore algorithm demonstrations, compare approaches, understand time and space complexity, and follow how an algorithm progresses from start to finish. This project is also a reflection of my own learning journey. Every feature is an opportunity to learn, experiment, and improve. The platform will continue to grow as I explore new algorithms, develop better visualizations, and discover simpler ways to explain technical concepts. Learn at your own pace. Understand the logic. Visualize the process. Because the best way to understand an algorithm is not just to read it, but to see it in action.
